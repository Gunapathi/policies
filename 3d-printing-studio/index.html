3D Printing Studio

An Android app by NETSTED (Netsted Infotech), Trichy, Tamil Nadu, India

3D Printing Studio helps you run a 3D printing business from your phone. It keeps your filament stock, orders, quotes, customers, prices and profit in one place, and it keeps them on your own device.

What the app does

  • Filament inventory — spools by material and colour, with stock in grams that orders use up as you print.
  • Orders and quotes — the items, printers, packing, delivery and finishing for each job, with the cost, your margin and the price worked out for you.
  • Customers — a simple customer list. Phone contacts can be copied in when you choose.
  • GST invoices — tax invoices as PDF, with CGST and SGST or IGST worked out from the details you enter.
  • Money and insights — every payment and refund, and your revenue and profit over any period.
  • Print farm — the printers at work, print jobs and maintenance.
  • Backups — local backups, and backups to your own Google Drive.

Your data stays on your phone

The app is local first: your records are stored on your device, not on our servers. Things leave your device only when you choose — a backup to your own Google Drive, an invoice or export you share, or activating your account. The privacy policy describes exactly what is stored and what is sent.

Why the app asks you to sign in with Google

Signing in is optional, and the app works without it. You sign in with Google only to:

  • Back up to your own Google Drive — the app uses its own folder there and cannot see anything else in your Drive.
  • Activate the Studio plan — your email address identifies your activation.

Before you sign in, the app asks you to read and accept the terms and conditions.

Plans

  • Base — the plan every account starts on, with limits on how many of some things you can make.
  • Studio — lifts those limits and unlocks more capabilities. It is arranged with the publisher.
